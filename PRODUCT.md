# Product Context

**Product Name**: E-commerce Analytics Platform
**Type**: Data-dense Admin Dashboard / Analytics
**Target Audience**: Sellers and business analysts extracting insights from Shopee ecommerce data.

**Key Features**:
- Global data filtering (time, price ranges, keywords)
- Micro-charts (sparklines) on KPI cards for quick trend analysis
- Dense, data-rich visualizations (Scatter charts, Rating distributions)
- Cross-filtering interactivity between charts and data tables
- CSV Export functionality for offline analysis

**Tone & Vibe**: Professional, analytical, growth-oriented, fast, and trustworthy.
